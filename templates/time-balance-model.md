# Time balance model (production and marketing)

## Principles
1. Production sets the ceiling; marketing sets the demand. Keep them within one lead time of each other.
2. Plan the hours; put marketing blocks in the calendar before production fills the week.
3. Batch: content, photos, listing updates and replies in blocks.
4. Cap the week (planned hours, hard cap, one day off).

## Rebalancing rules (4-week view)
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Idle capacity above 25% and orders below plan | Shift hours from production to marketing |
| Backlog above 2 weeks or missed ship dates | Shift hours to production or add help |
| Weekly hours above the cap for 2 weeks | Cut scope or hire; do not add hours |

Balance indicator: utilization = production hours needed by the order book / planned production hours (target 70% to 85%).
