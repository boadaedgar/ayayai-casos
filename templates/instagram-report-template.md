# Instagram report template (reports 1, 2 and 3)

Generic structure for a short Instagram account report. Target length: four to five pages. Time goals: report 1 in three hours or less, reports 2 and 3 in about 90 minutes each. Write for the owner, in plain language. Every number comes from a real source or says "no data".

## Report 1: where you are and what to do

1. **30-second summary:** where you are today, your biggest opportunity, the first thing we will do.
2. **Your business and goal:** from the interview, in the owner's words: what you do, ideal customer, two-month goal as a number, weekly time available, three words you repeat.
3. **Baseline table:** followers, reach (30 days), interactions (30 days), inquiries per month, posts per week. Columns: today, month-1 target, month-2 target. Source: the client's own Insights plus the public profile.
4. **Account diagnosis:** profile (bio, photo, link), content formats and which perform best, hooks (first line or first three seconds), calls to action, hashtags, consistency. Each point has one real example.
5. **Comparison:** the account against two competitors and one admired account on followers, posts per week, main format, hook type, average engagement. Close with three things to adapt.
6. **Recommendations (at most five, in priority order):** what, why, effort, impact.
7. **30-day plan:** four weekly checklists, sized to the hours the owner really has.
8. **How we will measure:** the same baseline metrics at month 1 and month 2.

## Reports 2 and 3: follow-up

1. **What changed:** the baseline table with a "now" column and the change.
2. **What you did and did not do:** checklist of the previous plan, done, partly done or not done, with the reason in the owner's words.
3. **What worked and what did not:** two or three standout posts since the last report, with why.
4. **Adjustments:** at most three changes for the next month.
5. **Plan for the next month:** a four-week checklist.
6. **Tools and resources:** a short table (what for, tool, how). Clients ask for this.
7. **How we will measure next:** the same table, with targets agreed with the owner.

Report 3 closes with a two-month summary and next steps the owner can do alone.

## Checklist before delivering
- [ ] Every example comes from a real post that can be opened.
- [ ] No invented number: no data is written as "no data".
- [ ] At most five recommendations (three adjustments in follow-ups).
- [ ] Account type, links and Insights verified in the client's own app.
- [ ] Sensitive client data does not leave the report.
- [ ] Time logged.

## Method note (put at the end of the report)
This report combines your interview and public Instagram data, analyzed with artificial intelligence and reviewed by a person. It does not guarantee results: they depend on applying the plan.
