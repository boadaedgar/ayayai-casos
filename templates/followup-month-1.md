# Month-1 follow-up

A short call plus a report that compares before and now, measures what the client applied, and sets the next 30 days.

## 1. Before the call
- Verify the account type, links and available Insights in the client's own app, not only from a scrape.
- Build the "baseline vs now" table with the same metrics as report 1. Write "no data" where there is none.
- Check which of the report 1 recommendations were applied, partly applied or not.
- Prepare the questions below and get consent for recording and for who attends.

## 2. The call (about 15 minutes)
| Block | Goal |
|---|---|
| Consent | Agree to the recording and to anyone else attending, on the recording |
| What is coming | Learn about launches or events; separate what was already planned from what came from the report |
| Applied or not | Go through the recommendations: which were easy, which were hard and why |
| Sustainability | Is the current posting rhythm realistic? What happens after the next launch? |
| Value | Which part helped most and least; would the client have paid for it (ask for an honest answer, a polite yes is not useful) |
| Referrals | One or two people with a similar need |
| Permission | Written consent for any use of the case |

## 3. Data to collect (last 30 days, from the client's Insights)
- Views and accounts reached, followers and net followers
- Interactions: likes, comments, saves, shares
- Content tab: top posts
- Views by content type, followers vs non-followers
- Profile visits and link taps
- Audience: gender and age

## 4. The report
Use [instagram-report-template.md](instagram-report-template.md), follow-up section: what changed, applied or not, what worked, at most three adjustments, a four-week checklist, tools, how to measure.

## 5. Close
- Written consent saved in the private archive.
- Referral contacts requested.
- Hours logged and the next report date set.

## Reading the data
- Compare reach with net followers: reach without followers means the content lacks a next step.
- Be careful with the newest posts: they have not matured, so do not compare their likes with older posts.
- Look for bursts: a posting burst can inflate numbers that do not hold.
