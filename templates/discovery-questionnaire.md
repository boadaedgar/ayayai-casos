# Discovery questionnaire (7 blocks)

Ask in order. Note each answer as stated, and mark unclear numbers for follow-up.

1. **What do you sell?** Products or services, how they differ, which one is the main focus, and what else you offer.
2. **Who do you buy from you?** Current customers by type; how you know them; ideal customer; if you had to lose one segment, which one.
3. **How do you price?** Price per unit, weight, hour or piece; what adds value; two or three recent orders with amounts; typical order size.
4. **Where do you sell?** Every channel, what each costs, how often you publish or update, and which one brings customers.
5. **What do you want to achieve, and in how long?** A number and a date; what the number means for the owner; reinvestment needed.
6. **How much real time can you give to marketing each week?** Include honest feelings about marketing: they shape which plan will actually be followed.
7. **What have you tried?** What worked, what did not, and why you think so; what the data shows.

Closing: if you could keep only one product, which one? Who could help you? What data can you send before the next session?
