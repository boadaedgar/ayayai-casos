# The ayayai business diagnostic: method

A repeatable process for turning a conversation with a small-business owner into a plan they can execute. Used in every case in this repository.

## 1. Discovery interview (about 60 to 75 minutes)
Seven blocks, in order: what is sold, to whom, price, where it is sold, the goal and its time frame, the real time available, and what has been tried. See [templates/discovery-questionnaire.md](templates/discovery-questionnaire.md). The session is recorded with permission and transcribed.

## 2. Structured brief
The transcript becomes a one-page brief: facts stated by the owner, our assumptions, and open data. Every figure is tagged as stated, estimate, assumption or pending. See [templates/business-brief.md](templates/business-brief.md).

## 3. Planning with AI in "plan mode"
The brief goes to one or more AI assistants with an instruction to ask for missing data and propose an approach before writing anything. Only after the approach is agreed does the assistant produce the plan. This keeps the model from filling gaps with invented facts.

## 4. Unit economics and funnel check
Before recommending growth spend, verify the numbers: does the stated margin match the price structure, how many orders are needed to reach the goal, how much traffic does that imply, and how many hours does each order consume.

## 5. Scenario comparison
Competing plans (for example, constrained versus focused) are compared on the same scenarios and a weighted decision matrix, with the conditions under which each one wins. See [templates/scenario-comparison.md](templates/scenario-comparison.md).

## 6. The plan
Written for its reader. Same content, different versions: a plain-language plan for the owner, and a version with assumptions, sensitivities and governance for a review group. Each contains the full year, a detailed first month, a day-by-day first week, what to learn and how, metrics, and rules for adjusting. See [templates/time-balance-model.md](templates/time-balance-model.md), [templates/weekly-scorecard.md](templates/weekly-scorecard.md), [templates/risk-register.md](templates/risk-register.md).

## 7. Measure and adjust
Weekly scorecard, monthly review, quarterly stage gates. Results are added to the case's update log at 30, 90 and 180 days, so the case shows what actually happened.

## Principles
- Test the constraints: remove one and see whether the plan changes.
- Never present an assumption as a fact.
- Plan hours, not hope.
- Be honest about status: a delivered plan is not a result.
