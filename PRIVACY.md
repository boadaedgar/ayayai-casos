# Privacy and consent policy

Everything in this repository is public. Before anything is added, ask: what happens if this becomes known to the client's customers and competitors tomorrow? If the answer is not "nothing", it does not go here.

## Consent
- Each client agrees to anonymized publication. Verbal consent on the recording is noted; before publishing, the client receives the draft and confirms in writing.
- A client can withdraw at any time. The case is removed from the repository and the Instagram post is taken down.

## What is removed or changed
1. Names, handles, shop names, URLs, locations, and the names of helpers or family members.
2. Dollar amounts and audience counts. Figures appear only as ratios, indexes or ranges, marked illustrative.
3. Product details that could identify the business (specific themes, brands, competitors).
4. Recordings, screenshots, faces, logos. File metadata is scrubbed.
5. Personal, health or political talk from the sessions.
6. Links to private documents.

## What is published
- The situation in generic terms.
- The method and the reasoning.
- Findings and trade-offs.
- Descriptions of the deliverables, not the deliverables.
- Reusable templates with no client data.
- Honest status, with results added later in an update log.

## Checks before every publication
- [ ] Banned-terms search (kept outside this repository) over the working tree and history returns nothing.
- [ ] No recordings, transcripts, documents or private links among tracked files.
- [ ] Images contain no names, handles, faces or logos.
- [ ] The client approved the final text in writing.
- [ ] Commit author is the GitHub no-reply address.
- [ ] Status wording matches reality (no unearned results).
